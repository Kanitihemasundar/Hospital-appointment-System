Free Health Checkups

Prevention is better than cure, and at HealthCare Plus, we are committed to promoting a culture of proactive health management. Our free health checkup camps are designed to make healthcare accessible to all, ensuring early detection and prevention of potential health issues.

  • Regular community health checkup camps
  • Comprehensive tests covering vital parameters like blood pressure, sugar levels, and cholesterol
  • Free consultation with expert healthcare professionals
  • Education on maintaining a healthy lifestyle

By offering free health checkups, we aim to empower individuals with the knowledge and resources to take charge of their well-being.
